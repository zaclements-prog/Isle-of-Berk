# Plan — Test Isle: a small island to play and look-dev on

**Why.** The game page is a flat 400 m test plane with coloured blocks. Flight is in now, and every interaction and
visual call needs a place with real terrain to run across, climb, take off from, fly over and land on: slopes,
ledges, beaches, cliffs, sea and trees. It is also where the look of the game gets iterated: terrain palette,
water, foliage, sky and fog.

**What.** A small northern island (~550 m across) in open sea. It reuses the Cove's terrain stack: the offline
heightfield bake, chunked LOD terrain, the six-layer splat material, and the Region contract. It adds an ocean,
seeded vegetation and boulders, and becomes the game page's default scene (`?scene=test` keeps the old plane).

## Island layout (`pipeline/terrain/islandShape.mjs`)
The grid is 1024² at 0.8 m (±409.6 m). +X is east, −Z north; the sea is at y = 0.
- **Coast:** an irregular outline, about 220–300 m from the centre, with a sheltered bay and lagoon in the south.
- **Beaches:** shingle beaches on the south and west, rising gently (~6 %).
- **Sea cliffs:** 25–35 m cliffs on the north-east, with 3 sea stacks offshore as flight and landing targets.
- **Hill:** a central peak of about 75 m, north-west of centre, with a ridge spur east and a stream valley running
  to the east beach.
- **Meadow:** a broad flat meadow in the south-west (≈ 12 m up, ~140 m across) for gallops, running takeoffs and
  landings. Spawn is here.
- **Ledges:** a stepped rock ledge flight (2.2 m risers, 3 m treads) at the meadow's edge for the climbing moves.
- **Erosion:** a light erosion pass, kept off the designed features (ledges, meadow, stacks).

## Bake outputs (`public/assets/world/island/`)
- `height.bin`, the uint16 heightfield, plus `splatA.png` and `splatB.png` with the Cove's layers: grass, forest,
  moss, mud, pebbles (shingle), rock, plus wet and AO.
- `terrain.json` with the grid, sea level, sun, spawn and seeded placements: trees (pines) and boulders, by the
  forest and rock masks and the slope.
- A preview image in `docs/progress/img/island/`.

## Runtime (`src/world/island/`)
- **`createIsland(app)`:** builds an `IslandRegion` (Region contract) from the terrain, ocean, trees and boulders,
  and sets the sun.
- **Ocean:** a large plane at sea level with a scrolling procedural normal map, depth-tinted by the terrain beneath
  through the height texture. Fog hides the horizon edge.
- **Trees:** instanced stylised pines (a trunk plus stacked cones, three variants). Their trunks are collision
  cylinders.
- **Boulders:** instanced noise-displaced rocks, with collision.
- **Game page:** loads the island, builds the `CollisionWorld` from its collision roots, spawns Toothless on the
  meadow, and calls `island.update` each frame.

## Gates
- Bake tests: determinism, the height range, the sea and beaches where expected, a flat enough meadow, ledge
  risers of 2.2 m, and stacks taller than 20 m.
- Runtime tests: region bounds and heights agree with the heightfield, collision roots present, placements on
  the ground.
- Captures of the game page on the island: spawn, a gallop and running takeoff over the meadow, the cliffs and
  stacks from the air.

## Revision: after Berk (2026-11-01)
After review, the island was redesigned to follow the films' Isle of Berk more closely, and grown to a 1 km grid at
1 m spacing (~800 m across).
- **Coast:** sea cliffs ring the island (15–40 m), with a forested western headland after Raven Point and sea stacks
  all round.
- **Mountain:** a massif in the north reaching ~175 m.
- **Harbour and village:**
  - A sheltered harbour channel in the south-east ends at a low shelf with three piers.
  - From the shelf, the village climbs the slope in five irregular, zigzagging terraces to the Great Hall on the
    mountain's flank. An earth path runs through every terrace.
  - Longhouses (turf and shingle roofs) sit in two rows per terrace, clear of the path.
- **The Kill Ring:** a round pit sunk into the cliff-top west of the village, with a stone kerb and a chain dome.
- **The Cove:** a hollow walled by rock in the western forest, with a gully way in and a pond (its own water plane).
- **Plateau:** a grassy cliff-top plateau in the east for gallops and running takeoffs, with the stepped tor on it.
- **Runtime additions:**
  - landmark geometry, with houses, the hall and the pier decks colliding;
  - grass only where the bake's splat says grass;
  - island-wide terrain collision (1 m to 270 m, 2 m beyond).
